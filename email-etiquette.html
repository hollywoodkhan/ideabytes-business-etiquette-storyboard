What is Email Etiquette? (The 4 Pillars)

Voiceover Narration

“Email is more than a way to share information. Every email we send represents us and the way we work with others. A clear, well-written email helps people understand our message, respond appropriately, and take the right action. On the other hand, a small mistake can lead to confusion, delays, or misunderstandings. Before you send, check four things: recipients — are you sending to the right people? Subject — does it make the purpose clear? Tone — is your message clear, polite and professional? And attachments — have you checked that the correct file is attached? These simple checks can make a real difference. Let’s see how they apply in a workplace situation.”

Cinematic Action & Veo Directives

Cut 1 (8.0s): Modern tech office in Bengaluru, sunlit workstations. Cut 2 (8.0s): Colleagues at standing huddle table reviewing tablet. Cut 3 (8.0s): OTS workstation with email client + Pillars 1 & 2 HUD (Recipients & Subject). Cut 4 (8.0s): Macro close-up hands typing + Pillars 3 & 4 HUD (Tone & Attachments). Cut 5 (8.0s): Collaborative office + All 4 Pillars HUD unified grid. Cut 6 (6.0s): Match-cut dolly push-in on protagonist Riya Kapoor at workstation (canonical anchor).

On-Screen Elements: Recipients — Send to the right people. • Subject — Make the purpose clear. • Tone — Be clear, polite and professional. • Attachments — Check that the correct file is attached.
